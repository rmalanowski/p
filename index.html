How to play

Turn the starting word into POOP in 6 moves or fewer.

  • Each move changes exactly one letter.
  • Every move has to be a real English word, and you can't reuse a word.
  • Your move is played as soon as you type the fourth letter. Invalid moves don't cost you a turn.
  • Stuck? Tap I suck to start over. It counts as a loss.
  • Every puzzle can be solved. Par is the fewest moves it can be done in.
